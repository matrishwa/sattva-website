Welcome to Sattva

At Sattva Foods & Wellness, we believe that nourishment begins with purity. Every meal, every product, and every gift we create is a reflection of our commitment to wholesome living and traditional wellness.

We serve families, professionals, seniors, and wellness-conscious individuals throughout the Greater Toronto Area with fresh, homemade-style vegetarian meals and handcrafted wellness foods. Our roots are in tradition. Our focus is on your well-being.

Sattva means "purity" and "essence." That's what we deliver to your table.

Wellness Foods & Handcrafted Products

Traditional wellness foods made with care and pure ingredients

Beyond our fresh meal service, Sattva offers handcrafted wellness foods rooted in traditional Indian nutrition. These are time-honoured foods that support everyday well-being, made with the same care and purity as our tiffin service.

🌾

Panjiri

A warming traditional mix with whole grains, ghee and dry fruits. A comfort food for wellness and energy.

🔘

Gondh & Dry-Fruit Ladoo

Handcrafted balls of nourishment with edible gum, nuts and traditional ingredients.

🌿

Alsi Seeds

Whole flaxseeds packed with natural goodness. A staple of traditional wellness.

🌱

Methi

Pure fenugreek seeds, traditionally valued for everyday well-being.

☀️

Til & Jaggery Products

Sesame and jaggery combinations, handmade and packed with traditional warmth.

🥥

Coconut Burfi

A delicate, handcrafted sweet treat made with pure coconut and ghee.

🫓

Traditional Parathas

Handmade parathas made fresh with pure ingredients and desi ghee.

🍂

Seasonal Wellness Foods

Rotating selection of seasonal products, always fresh, always mindful.

Our wellness foods are traditional foods made with care and purity. They are not medical products and do not make health-treatment claims. We celebrate the traditional role of these foods in everyday well-being and conscious living.
