Wellness Foods & Handcrafted Products

Traditional wellness foods made with care and pure ingredients

Beyond our fresh meal service, Sattva offers handcrafted wellness foods rooted in traditional Indian nutrition. These are time-honoured foods that support everyday well-being, made with the same care and purity as our tiffin service.

🌾

Panjiri

A rich and nourishing traditional Panjiri prepared with carefully selected ingredients, desi ghee, dry fruits, seeds and aromatic spices. A wholesome, flavourful treat inspired by traditional Indian recipes.

🍫

Coconut & Dry Fruit Barfi

A delicious handmade barfi prepared with coconut and selected dry fruits. Soft, rich and naturally satisfying—perfect for those who enjoy traditional homemade sweets.

🎁

Peda (पेड़ा)

A traditional milk-based sweet made with rich, wholesome ingredients and a delicate nutty finish. A satisfying treat for moments when you want something naturally indulgent.

🔘

Gond Laddu

A traditional Indian laddu made with edible gond, nuts and wholesome ingredients. Rich, hearty and naturally satisfying—perfect as an energizing traditional snack.

💪

Protein Panjiri

A traditional-style panjiri prepared with protein-rich ingredients, nuts, seeds and wholesome grains. A nutrient-dense snack designed to provide sustained energy and nourishment.

🌿

Traditional Methi Wellness Ladoo

A traditional recipe featuring methi seeds combined with carefully selected ingredients, nuts and desi ghee. Rich in flavour and inspired by generations of homemade wellness foods.

🌱

Alsi (Flaxseed) Wellness Ladoo

Wholesome handmade ladoos prepared with roasted flaxseeds, traditional ingredients and a delicious balance of natural flavours. A satisfying treat for everyday enjoyment.

🥜

Moong Dal Aata Burfi

A nourishing combination of moong dal and whole-wheat flour, enriched with nuts and traditional ingredients. A wholesome, energy-giving sweet with a satisfying texture.

✨

Premium Dry Fruit Energy Ladoo

A premium blend of carefully selected dry fruits and wholesome ingredients, handcrafted into delicious, rich and satisfying ladoos. A perfect treat for gifting or enjoying with family.

☀️

Til (Sesame) & Jaggery Ladoo

Traditional handmade ladoos prepared with roasted sesame seeds and jaggery for a deliciously nutty and wholesome flavour. A classic traditional treat enjoyed especially during the colder months.

👶

Lactation Laddu

A traditional blend of wholesome ingredients, nuts and seeds, crafted as a nourishing snack for mothers during the lactation period. Rich, satisfying and thoughtfully prepared to support everyday nourishment and wellness.

Our wellness foods are traditional foods made with care and purity. They are not medical products and do not make health-treatment claims. We celebrate the traditional role of these foods in everyday well-being and conscious living.
